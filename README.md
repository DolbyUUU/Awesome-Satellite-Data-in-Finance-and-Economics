# Satellite Data Applications in Finance and Economics 📡💹

A curated list of academic papers on the applications of **satellite data (satellite imagery)** in **finance and economics**. It serves as a resource for researchers and practitioners exploring the intersection of satellite technology and economic/financial analysis.

---

## 📘 Paper List

### Economics (Most are Surveys)
- **A Generalizable and Accessible Approach to Machine Learning with Global Satellite Imagery**
- **Big Data as a Governance Mechanism**  
- **NIGHT LIGHTS IN ECONOMICS SOURCES AND USES**  
- **On the Capital Market Consequences of Big Data: Evidence from Outer Space**  
- **Space, the Final Economic Frontier**  
- **The View from Above: Applications of Satellite Data in Economics**  

### Finance (Mostly Quant Trading)
- **Does Alternative Data Improve Financial Forecasting? The Horizon Effect**  
- **Institutional Trading and Satellite Data**  
- **The Firm Next Door: Using Satellite Images to Study Local Information Advantage**  
- **The Use and Usefulness of Big Data in Finance: Evidence from Financial Analysts**  
- **VENTURE CAPITAL IN THE NEWSPACE ECONOMY**
